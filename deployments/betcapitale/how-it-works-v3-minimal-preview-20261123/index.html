HOW IT WORKS01 / 04
BEHIND EVERY COUPON

Simple to follow.
Careful by design.

Each stage has a specific purpose. No complicated dashboard is needed to understand the method.

01
SOFTWARE

First, we analyse.

Football matches are reviewed through market movements, team information and statistical context. The software looks for selections that meet defined criteria.

The starting point is data — not a guess.
INPUT Match & market information
02
MATCHES

Then, we filter.

Not every match becomes a selection. Only qualifying opportunities move forward, each with a clearly identified market and preference.

Fewer selections. Clearer decisions.
OUTPUT Selected matches & markets
03
COUPONS

Next, we build.

Selections are distributed across coupons. Each coupon receives an equal stake, and the day's total exposure is considered as one strategy.

A coordinated set, not isolated picks.
OUTPUT Coupons with odds & stakes
04
RESULTS

Finally, we record.

When matches finish, coupon outcomes are settled. Winning and losing coupons are counted together to calculate the actual daily balance.

The full result matters more than any one coupon.
OUTPUT Recorded daily balance
THE CORE IDEA

Not every coupon
needs to win.

A single winning coupon doesn't guarantee a profitable day. A losing coupon doesn't tell the whole story either. What matters is the total result after accounting for every stake.

THE DAILY BALANCE
Total returns
−all coupon stakes
= Daily net result

Some days are positive. Others are negative. Neither is guaranteed.
