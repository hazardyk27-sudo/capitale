THE DAILY APPROACH

The result is the
whole day.

CAPITALE does not measure the day by how many coupons win. It measures what came back compared with everything staked.

HOW THE CALCULATION WORKS
Total returns−Total stakes=Net result
IN THIS EXAMPLE
€270−€200=+€70

On another day, returns could be lower than stakes. A loss is always possible.

TRANSPARENCY

after publishing?

The coupon record starts before kickoff. After the matches finish, its outcome is added. The history should show both winning and losing records.
