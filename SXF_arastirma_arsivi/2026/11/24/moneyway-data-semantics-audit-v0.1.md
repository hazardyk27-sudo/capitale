## Consequences
Moneyway share is the share of matched activity by runner, not percentage of bettors backing the runner or directional net investment. Every exchange match has a back and lay counterparty, so totals cannot identify aggressor, information, bettor count, or smart money. Rising matched volume + falling odds describes a price/volume pattern; not proof of buying pressure. Last traded prices can be stale/asynchronous and not executable; normalizing across outcomes does not solve stale quotes or bid/ask spread.
FLOW RECOVERY remains an observed pattern, not proof of informed backing. Absolute volume can measure activity; concentration and acceleration can measure distribution/activity; direction requires price and preferably synchronized executable order-book data. Do not reinterpret older reports as independent directional proof.
