# FINAL Source Policy

Status: DRAFT
Version: 0.1

## Allowed inputs

FINAL may use completed CAPITALE department reports only:
- ANALYST
- NEWS
- SXF
- POLY

## Prohibited

FINAL must not:
- browse the web for new match evidence;
- query raw football, news, SmartXFlow, or Polymarket data to repair a report;
- import outside picks or market commentary;
- silently infer missing department evidence.

## Freshness and completeness

FINAL must preserve each report's freshness/data-quality status. A stale, missing, or unreliable department input is a limitation, not neutral evidence.

Current architecture expects all four department reports before a normal final decision. Any future degraded-mode exception requires explicit approval and versioning.
