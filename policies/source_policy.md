# CAPITALE General Source Policy

Status: CANONICAL
Version: 1.0

## Principles

1. Use the source domain assigned to the active role only.
2. Prefer primary/direct evidence over summaries.
3. Record source, timestamp, freshness, and claim supported.
4. Separate direct fact from interpretation.
5. Do not use one department's report as another department's evidence.
6. Do not fill missing evidence with assumptions.
7. Conflicts between credible sources must be retained and surfaced.
8. Low-confidence sources may create a research lead but cannot alone support a material conclusion.
9. Current information must satisfy the role's freshness requirements.
10. Historical data must be comparable to the period being analyzed.

## Contamination rule

If a role accidentally encounters prohibited information, it must ignore that information for its report and avoid allowing it to influence interpretation.

## Evidence record

Each material claim should be traceable to:
- source or dataset;
- observed/fetched time;
- event/match/wallet identifier where relevant;
- raw or derived status;
- confidence/quality status;
- methodology version for derived metrics.

## Market/news separation

Football-performance evidence, current news, SmartXFlow flow, and Polymarket wallet intelligence are separate evidence classes. They must remain isolated until FINAL.

## Reliability over completeness

An incomplete report with explicit uncertainty is preferred over a complete-looking report built on weak or contaminated evidence.
