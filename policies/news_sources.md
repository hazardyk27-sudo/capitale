# NEWS Source Policy

Status: CANONICAL
Version: 1.4
Applicable methodology: NEWS MATCH IMPACT v1.6

## Mandatory source directory (2026-11-24)

Read `policies/news_source_directory.md` at startup. It defines the named international and 19 domestic country routes, verified entry-access caveats, dynamic official club/organizer sources, local beat reporting and supporter research. NEWS performs all research itself. Start with both clubs' direct sources and local-language reporting; use international coverage as relevant corroboration. For cross-border fixtures, research both club countries. For smaller clubs and unlisted jurisdictions, discover suitable local sources independently. Use the directory as a discovery guide, not a closed whitelist or mandatory list of every outlet. Log actual searches, access limits, original provenance and source gaps in the full report. Missing individual publishers never blocks starting research; seek permitted alternatives. Never bypass restrictions or treat headline/snippet alone as a verified material fact. N1–N4 grades apply to each claim, not automatically to every article from a listed outlet. Existing eight-area scope, relevance gate and v1.6 scoring remain unchanged.

## Source grades — preserve CAPITALE baseline

- **N1** official/direct primary information: clubs, manager/player primary statements, verified supporter association's own announcement of its actions, competition/federation, documented venue/weather source. A fan group's announcement verifies *its announcement/action*, not every supporter’s opinion or an on-field effect.
- **N2** credible direct local beat/established journalist reporting: sourced manager/player coverage, training, supporter demonstrations, internal club changes and match atmosphere.
- **N3** contextual and observed fan sentiment: traceable direct fan discussion/forum threads/quotes and local commentary, used with time, sampling and selection-bias caveats. A few commenters do not represent an entire fan base. This is generally insufficient alone for a large directional effect.
- **N4** speculation, rumor, isolated anonymous posts, low-quality aggregators. Research leads only; must not independently earn points.

## Relevance filter for fan/stadium content

**Source authenticity does not equal football relevance.** Filter out routine supporter-transport buses, match-day meeting points, promotions, normal ticket sales/sellouts, generic cheering, fan-zone planning and common supporter displays. These must not be H# scored news or be used to infer a team-performance or goal-market advantage; do not even include them as filler with 0 points.

Material exceptions require independent, current verification AND an explicit match-specific causal mechanism: substantial boycott/protest, major stadium/attendance restriction, incidents affecting the match or preparations, or similarly exceptional crowd developments. Separate the directly verified event from conjectured impact and allow unscored reporting if effect remains unsubstantiated.

Still **search** supporter/stadium news for both teams; `NO_MATERIAL_FAN_NEWS` is an acceptable result even when routine fan activity exists.

## Mandatory breadth

For BOTH teams, research manager statements; organized fan/stadium atmosphere and direct sentiment; local press/team climate; player interviews/reactions; unusual stakes/derby; club management/off-field; squad absences/returns/mitigation; travel/rest/practice and material weather/pitch. Record verified news rather than unrelated background. If a topic has no credible material results, do not fabricate a development.

## Evidence and attribution

Every scored H# story needs at least one direct traceable source and an AS_OF-valid publication time if determinable. Mark uncertain timing or contested reports; do not knowingly use information first available after AS_OF. Cluster reposts or multiple publications of the same underlying story to avoid double counting. Distinguish what a source actually says from the assistant's plausible but unverified football interpretation.

Do not score generic media hype, routine home advantage or broad fan optimism as though they guarantee goals/wins. Credible supporter reactions may be reported without directional points. If evidence is inadequate, status is NO_EVIDENCE, not observed neutrality.

## v1.5 clarification

N1-N4 source credibility and the material match-impact fan-event filter remain unchanged. The user-selection /100 support number is a separate judgment based on already cited material NEWS evidence, its reliability and credible on-field mechanism—not an automatic 10-point conversion of signed NEWS totals. It never incorporates odds, SmartXFlow, other departments or fan publicity without direct match relevance.

## Prohibited sources

No bookmaker odds, betting-market flow, SmartXFlow, Polymarket, ANALYST or other CAPITALE department reports. User-supplied odds, money percentages and engine labels must not be used as NEWS evidence.
