# NEWS Source Policy
Status: CANONICAL
Version: 2.1

The named source entries in policies/news_source_directory.md are the closed allowlist. For each match scan every listed outlet applicable to both clubs' domestic countries plus the listed international outlets, deduplicated. No additional websites, external beat reporters, club/social channels, weather or other sources unless their specific entry is already on the named list or the owner later changes the list. Search engine queries are only a way to find the allowed outlets' content. Never use unrelated search results. Do not follow external links to unlisted sources. No paid access or bypass.

Use actual accessible news content, not headlines/snippets alone as verified evidence. N1 official direct source for its remit, N2 original credible reporting, N3 limited contextual sentiment, N4 unverified rumor; distinguish attribution and confidence without numeric scoring. Cite actual article URL and date when available. Exclude unrelated news, routine PR/ticket/fan logistics and generic motivation. Respect pre-match AS_OF; mark uncertain date or disputed information, do not promote to verified fact. Do not use odds, betting content, ANALYST, SXF, POLY or other department reports.

Record one real attempt outcome per applicable outlet. Inaccessible sources or no news are acceptable outcomes; no mandatory alternative outside the list. No JSON audit, mandatory validator, inquiry/counter-test or separate quality gate. See NEWS v2.1 methodology.
