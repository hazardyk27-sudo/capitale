## Mandatory discovery and routing

- This is the single registry for the 14 owner-selected editorial sources. The four international sources apply to any fixture; choose local sources by the domestic competition country (for cross-border cups, both teams' domestic countries). Greece has two registered local sources. Other countries have no registered local source in this shortlist.
- Search the registered site's domain with both team names (including local spellings), competition and fixture date in its native language. Locate the specific pre-match article, not just the home page. Record FOUND / NOT_FOUND / INACCESSIBLE / EXCLUDED with article URL, author, publication timestamp and reasons. Use public material only; no paywall bypass.
- First freeze the independent statistical baseline, then use the four international sources and applicable local sources through the separate claim-only extraction boundary. Never browse raw betting articles in the ANALYST context or treat this list as permission to use betting content. Missing coverage is not evidence against a team. Do not require every publisher to cover every fixture.
- No source ranking is a reliability score; verify each claim. Exclude AI-generated predictions and unverified human authorship from the human-editorial category. Editorial material supplements rather than replaces A1/A2 primary statistical evidence.

Access, authorship, original provenance, publication time, coverage, and usage terms must be checked per article. Inclusion here is not an assertion of universal free access, accuracy, independence or predictive skill.

## Mandatory anti-anchoring protocol

1. Independent baseline: ANALYST completes and freezes its own allowed statistical and tactical evidence and initial factual interpretation before receiving editorial extracts. Record as_of and baseline hash.
2. Separate extraction boundary: an upstream extractor, not the same ANALYST context, supplies structured claim-only extracts. Remove explicit and implicit betting recommendations, predicted scores, betting odds, model probabilities, confidence, betting market statistics, ranking of selections, and promotional calls to action. If filtering cannot be established, do not pass the article to ANALYST. Prompt instructions alone do not guarantee blindness.
3. Source separation: current news, injuries, squad availability and rumors must not enter ANALYST; any such material is reserved for NEWS, subject to its own independent approved policy. No SXF or POLY material. Preserve source isolation.
4. For each claim record source URL, author if available, publication/update time, claim type (observed fact or editorial interpretation), supporting data, underlying provenance and uncertainty. Reject future/post-kickoff information. Check usage terms; no paywall bypass.
5. Independently verify factual claims from allowed football sources. Treat unverified tactical interpretation as a hypothesis, not a verified fact. Seek counterevidence and explain limitations. No automatic agreement with the author.
6. Deduplicate repeated claims and shared data sources. Multiple publishers repeating one fact are not independent confirmations. Never use publisher vote counts or recommendation consensus.
7. Log baseline versus post-extract changes and the concrete evidence that justified each change; retain disagreements and rejected claims. No directional change solely due to editorial authority.
8. FINAL receives verified findings, clearly marked interpretations, limitations and contradictions, not third-party picks or scores. ANALYST v3.0 emits no score, pick or forecast; NEWS and FINAL methods otherwise unchanged.
9. Test baseline-only versus blinded editorial augmentation on timestamped historical matches, with frozen outputs and separate settlement; measure incremental value, contamination, coverage and reproducibility as prospective evaluation; do not claim a performance gain before validation.

This registry operates under AGENTS.md, policies/analyst_sources.md and the active v3.0 methodology. A separate extractor must demonstrate isolated claim-only input; if unavailable or contaminated, BLOCKED. No raw prediction articles in ANALYST context. Record every applicable source attempt; genuine no coverage is a disclosed limitation. No automatic deployment or enforcement is asserted.
