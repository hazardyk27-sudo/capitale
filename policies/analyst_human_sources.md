## Mandatory discovery and routing

- This is the single registry for the 14 owner-selected editorial sources. All 14 sources are applicable to EVERY fixture, irrespective of country, league or tournament. Country/language and listed domestic coverage are discovery hints only, never exclusion rules. A German, Greek, Italian or other editorial outlet may cover a match involving unrelated countries.
- After DATA freeze, search across all 14 registered domains for the exact fixture using both team names, aliases/local spellings, competition and date. Batch domain-targeted queries in supported tools to minimize latency. A batch must actually cover each registered source; a broad search that returns only a few domains is not evidence of searching the others. Open only relevant candidate articles, not every site's homepage or unrelated matches. If a relevant article is found, inspect its permitted substantive text; if discovery is ambiguous, refine the targeted search. Locate the specific pre-match article, not just the home page. Record FOUND / NOT_FOUND / INACCESSIBLE / EXCLUDED with article URL, author, publication timestamp and reasons. Use public material only; no paywall bypass.
- First freeze the independent statistical baseline, then the same ANALYST searches all 14 registered sources for fixture-specific coverage. Prefer filtered/clean football sections. Mixed pages may be consulted only for separable permitted football observations; ignore and log accidental exposure without reproducing forecasts or using them. Exclude inseparable claims. Missing coverage is not evidence against a team. HUMAN is not a department or agent.
- No source ranking is a reliability score; verify each claim. Exclude AI-generated predictions and unverified human authorship from the human-editorial category. Editorial material supplements rather than replaces A1/A2 primary statistical evidence.

Access, authorship, original provenance, publication time, coverage, and usage terms must be checked per article. Inclusion here is not an assertion of universal free access, accuracy, independence or predictive skill.

## Mandatory anti-anchoring protocol

1. Independent baseline: ANALYST completes and freezes its own allowed statistical and tactical evidence and initial factual interpretation before receiving editorial extracts. Record as_of and baseline hash.
2. Claim filtering: by ANALYST after baseline freeze. Remove explicit and implicit recommendations, predicted scores, betting odds/probabilities, confidence, market rankings and promotional content from the evidence and report. Prefer retrieval that omits such content; if encountered, log source/category and mitigation, not the prohibited text. Reading editorial material may introduce bias; report residual anchoring risk.
3. Source separation: current news, injuries, squad availability and rumors must not be used by ANALYST; any such material is reserved for NEWS, subject to its own independent approved policy. No SXF or POLY material. Preserve source isolation.
4. For each claim record source URL, author if available, publication/update time, claim type (observed fact or editorial interpretation), supporting data, underlying provenance and uncertainty. Reject future/post-kickoff information. Check usage terms; no paywall bypass.
5. Independently verify factual claims from allowed football sources. Treat unverified tactical interpretation as a hypothesis, not a verified fact. Seek counterevidence and explain limitations. No automatic agreement with the author.
6. Deduplicate repeated claims and shared data sources. Multiple publishers repeating one fact are not independent confirmations. Never use publisher vote counts or recommendation consensus.
7. Log baseline versus post-extract changes and the concrete evidence that justified each change; retain disagreements and rejected claims. No directional change solely due to editorial authority.
8. FINAL receives verified findings, clearly marked interpretations, limitations and contradictions, not third-party picks or scores. ANALYST v3.0 emits no score, pick or forecast; NEWS and FINAL methods otherwise unchanged.
9. Test baseline-only versus blinded editorial augmentation on timestamped historical matches, with frozen outputs and separate settlement; measure incremental value, contamination, coverage and reproducibility as prospective evaluation; do not claim a performance gain before validation.

This registry operates under AGENTS.md, policies/analyst_sources.md and v3.0.5 methodology. Claim-extraction protocol: prompts/analyst_human_extract.md. HUMAN is a source registry only. Record every actual registered source attempt; exclude contaminated/unsupported claims and continue, with explicit limitations. No claim of complete immunity to editorial bias.
