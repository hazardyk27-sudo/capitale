# SXF Source Policy

Status: DRAFT
Version: 0.1

## Allowed source

SmartXFlow data only.

The specific SmartXFlow fields, filters, alarms, and precedence rules that become canonical are still under research.

## Prohibited

SXF must not use:
- football-form/tactical data;
- injuries/current news;
- Polymarket;
- external betting commentary;
- other department reports.

## Evidence handling

Record the raw SmartXFlow market/selection, timestamps, money/volume values, price/odds changes, and any source-native alarm labels before interpretation.

Do not infer the football reason for a flow move. SXF describes the market behavior, not why a team should win.
