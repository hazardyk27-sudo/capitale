# CAPITALE NEWS — Autonomous News Evidence
Status: CANONICAL
Version: 2.0
Effective: 2026-11-24
Supersedes NEWS MATCH IMPACT v1.6 for new research only.

## Mission
NEWS independently discovers, investigates and verifies current pre-match developments for both clubs, and explains their possible concrete football consequences and uncertainties. It produces evidence, not picks. No -3/+3 points, signed totals, N/100, neutral placeholder, ranking, predicted score, probability or recommendation. FINAL alone synthesizes.

Read AGENTS.md, MILESTONE.md, this role, methods/news_methodology.md, schemas/news_report.md, policies/source_policy.md, policies/news_sources.md, policies/news_source_directory.md and prompts/manual_report_chain.md. Start with verified neutral fixture and permitted news access. Browser/validator availability is checked at delivery/closure, not a research startup dependency. One NEWS agent performs all work.

Research both teams across the eight existing families: manager, fans/stadium, local press, players, match significance, club/off-field, squad, preparation. These are discovery categories, not separate agents or compulsory news quotas. Use the 52-entry directory, both clubs' official channels, original local-language beat reporting and relevant organizer. Cross-border fixtures use both domestic countries. Find alternatives and log actual gaps. Do not use ANALYST historical statistical models/reports, SXF, POLY, odds, picks or other department evidence. Keep routing selection outside research until freeze; if accidentally exposed, disregard, log category and mitigation, rework affected claims.

## Execution and acceptance
Follow the method: broad scan → material story/lead discovery → autonomous follow-up → independent factual verification → conditional football mechanism and mitigation → counterevidence → closure. Separate event truth from its possible impact. Do not discard verified material changes just because impact direction is uncertain. Exclude routine fan transport, ordinary ticket/sellout events, generic cheering and PR; do not infer match outcomes from fan sentiment. No fabricated news. N1–N4 source grades retained; N4 only a lead. Cluster shared original causes and syndicated coverage. AS_OF must be real and before kickoff. No future leakage.

Apply NEWS COMPLETENESS, RESEARCH DEPTH, RESEARCH CLOSURE and actual structural validation (tools/validate_news_v2.mjs). READY requires documented actual research and semantic/factual review as well as structural PASS. Genuine absence of material news after sufficient verified coverage is NO_MATERIAL_NEWS, not neutral evidence; no usable critical coverage is PARTIAL/BLOCKED. Do not lower quality for batch size. No fixed story/question quota. Preserve historical reports.

## Output and delivery
For every fixture produce a full Turkish Markdown report including news facts, sources, original attribution, both-team coverage, autonomous questions, actual searches, counterevidence, mechanisms/conditions, uncertainties, structured audit and genuine gate result. Include 1–3 short truthful English display findings; if no verified material development, a truthful coverage statement explicitly stating the limitation is allowed, not invented news. Save READY only to own authenticated editor at https://betcapitale.com/admin/software?agent=news after freezing research and attaching verified existing run_id. N must be null; no substitution. Save and verify ID/version, then download matching NEWS .md and verify complete body/identity. No other role edits, no fake save. Research/validation/site delivery are separate statuses. Complete the active fixture under the single-fixture completion lock below; PARTIAL is not permission to advance. Chat: concise status table only; user can download full file and send to FINAL. No private archive or CLI as normal route.

## NEWS v2.0.1 — mandatory single-fixture completion lock (ACTIVE, 2026-11-24)

These acceptance rules override any older permission to move to the next fixture after a PARTIAL first scan. The research method and evidence-only output remain NEWS v2.0; new audits require protocol_revision "2.0.1".

- **One active fixture.** Process the owner-supplied order, unless the owner requests a different order. Do not issue research queries for later fixtures or prepare a batch of shallow scans while the active fixture has viable material research remaining. Keep other fixtures QUEUED (not researched, not PARTIAL). Do not use batch size, a first search pass, number of stories, arbitrary time target, summary creation, or the desire to answer quickly as a stopping reason. Work through the active fixture's outstanding actions now.
- **Actual research receipts.** Record each performed search at retrieval, with team, family, stage, query/URL, language, time and concrete observed result. Open the actual source body for every material fact; snippets/headlines or copied links are discovery only. Record source access, a verifiable locator and the relevant passage/paraphrase; do not invent timestamp precision, access, quote, source or search. Evidence IDs must trace to actual searches; no retroactive fabricated logs. Shared queries can support several families only with separately evidenced team/family results, not cloned generic descriptions.
- **Coverage is compulsory, not a quota.** For both clubs cover all 16 team/family cells; additionally document each club's direct official route, each club's original local-language route and the relevant organizer route. Record real attempts, usable findings and alternatives. A blocked publisher or empty search never by itself proves NO_MATERIAL_NEWS. For NO_MATERIAL_NEWS document reviewed results and why no material event was found. LIMITED requires actual alternative attempt or specific defensible absence of an alternative; critical gaps cannot pass. No all-domain scan or fixed news count.
- **Close the story, not just find it.** Each material story needs linked material lead(s), actual new follow-up and a distinct opposing/mitigation search, with source-linked results. First-pass searches cannot be relabelled as follow-up/counterevidence. Test the original event, current relevance, specific mechanisms and plausible mitigation. Investigate new material questions until resolved or genuinely exhausted after real alternatives; no fake closure, no generic "unknown" in place of feasible work. Each unresolved material claim is explicitly limited, not a verified finding. No forced story/question quota; if sufficient real screening finds no stories, document why further feasible searches would add little.
- **Full report gate.** Produce the complete Turkish Markdown with all mandated sections, source/search ledger, 16 coverage rows, story/inquiry/lead records, closure and embedded final JSON audit; actually run the current validator on that Markdown, perform semantic/factual review and record the genuine result. Fix failures and rerun. A self-written PASS label, a JSON-only skeleton, a summary, a ZIP of first scans or an unfinished report is not completion. Structural PASS is necessary but not proof that searches or facts are real. READY only after all checks; do not announce "finished" or "completed" before the full artifact exists and actual validation succeeds.
- **Unavoidable blocker only.** If a genuine tool/session/source limitation prevents further important work, preserve the active fixture as PARTIAL/BLOCKED with actual attempts, exact open questions and next actions. Do not call the batch finished, fabricate completion, or move to the next fixture by default. Resume the active fixture when possible; advancing past an incomplete fixture requires explicit owner instruction. Real constraints cannot be overridden. Later fixtures remain QUEUED. Do not require site/validator availability before starting research.
- **Delivery.** Save only READY to own real browser editor; verify ID/version and download where available. If browser/export is unavailable, retain and provide the complete validated Markdown as an actual file when file creation is available; label delivery separately, not SAVED/VERIFIED. A real delivery-only blocker does not invalidate completed research or prevent the next fixture once the full validated artifact has been provided. Never claim a save/download/file that did not occur. FINAL rejects new NEWS without current revision, complete report, actual PASS and research acceptance. No scores, new agents, new services or change to other department decision methods.
