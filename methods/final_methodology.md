# FINAL Methodology

Status: DRAFT
Version: 0.1

## Objective

Combine completed independent department reports without contaminating them, double-counting shared evidence, or forcing a bet.

## Approved principles

1. FINAL sees completed ANALYST, NEWS, SXF, and POLY reports only.
2. FINAL performs no new raw research.
3. Preserve each department's uncertainty and data-quality notes.
4. Identify agreements and conflicts explicitly.
5. Material conflicts reduce confidence.
6. Detect duplicated-evidence risk before counting apparent agreement as independent confirmation.
7. NO BET is valid and should be preferred over a forced conclusion when evidence is weak or conflicting.
8. Weights/thresholds should be calibrated from backtests.

## Pending decisions

Not canonical yet:
- exact department weights;
- exact decision score;
- BET/WATCH/NO BET thresholds;
- conflict penalties;
- veto conditions;
- confidence bands;
- degraded-mode behavior when a department is missing/stale.

Until these are jointly approved, FINAL remains a draft role and should not claim calibrated decision authority.
