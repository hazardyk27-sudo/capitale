# CAPITALE ANALYST — Autonomous Evidence Research

Status: CANONICAL
Version: 3.0
Operational revision: 3.0.2 — single ANALYST, two source categories
Effective: 2026-11-24
Owner-approved: standard baseline + human insight + autonomous deep research + critical review + evidence-only report.
SUPERSEDES v2.1 and all earlier ANALYST methods for NEW runs. No historical revision.

## Non-negotiable principles
Research the whole fixture symmetrically; never start from a desired outcome. Only allowed football-performance evidence, not current news, squad availability, odds, market predictions, SXF/POLY or another department. No recommendation, ordinal score, win probability, predicted score or directional lean in any ANALYST output. FINAL alone decides. Actual data and reproducible source traces are mandatory; plausible language is not evidence.

## 0. Preflight
Read current startup files and verify neutral fixture, competition, season, kickoff and pre-match AS_OF. Begin DATA as soon as permitted data access exists. Do not require a second agent, separate session, browser login, validator runtime or server deployment before research. Historic replay requires pre-cutoff evidence snapshots, not today's data. Keep routing separate until freeze; if selection or prohibited content is accidentally seen, record exposure type, ignore it and discard/rework affected claims. Do not pretend to have context isolation. Only actual missing critical research data/identity prevents research; validator and site are checked at their respective later stages.

## 1. DATA — independent baseline
Use the 18-source DATA registry, prioritizing official records and appropriate metrics, not all sites mechanically. Establish both teams' comparable season baseline, recent form, opponent quality, home/away, goals and underlying attack/defense metrics; inspect possession/style and relevant game-state information when sourced. Season, recent five/three/one windows and strength/venue context are useful starting points, not fixed analytical weights or mandatory report pillars. Include actual sample sizes, match identifiers, provider, time windows, source and missing-data statuses. Do not omit inconvenient fixtures; explain exclusions and comparability (competition, red cards, coaching-era boundaries when historical and allowed). Distinguish results from performance; never infer tactics from formation or possession alone. Save immutable baseline evidence and initial interpretation with timestamp and snapshot/hash before any HUMAN material is available. No picks or rankings.

## 2. HUMAN — single-agent claim discovery, not an authority
Use policies/analyst_human_sources.md and prompts/analyst_human_extract.md. After DATA baseline freeze, the SAME ANALYST searches the four international sources and relevant local sources (both countries for cross-border fixtures). HUMAN is a source category only. Prefer clean statistical/tactical passages or filtered retrieval; if a mixed editorial page is opened, extract only separable permitted factual/tactical observations. Never use or reproduce forecasts, betting picks, odds, confidence, consensus, current news or squad information. If encountered, record exposure category/source without content, discard it and counter-test the remaining claim from independent permitted DATA. If a claim cannot be separated, exclude it. Record actual source attempts, author, date, URL, reasons and limitations. No usable articles after actual discovery is a disclosed limitation, not a blocker. Reading human-authored commentary carries residual anchoring risk; independent baseline, verification and counterevidence remain mandatory.

## 3. AUTONOMOUS — discover, ask, test, adapt
The baseline and sanitized HUMAN claims are leads, not conclusions. Identify meaningful discrepancies, unexpected patterns, uncertainties, matchup mechanisms and possible causal alternatives. Independently generate your own fixture-specific research questions; no prescribed list of questions, mechanical factor scores or arbitrary quota of searches. Prioritize questions that could materially change understanding of the match. For each question record the trigger evidence, hypothesis and competing explanation, concrete search/query or data comparison actually executed, new evidence, result and remaining uncertainty. Investigate new questions arising from results, iterating DATA → questions → evidence → updated understanding; HUMAN may be re-consulted only through the same claim-only filtering and exposure-log rules. If no meaningful new question exists, document the actual screening and evidence-based stopping reason; do not fabricate questions or research. If material questions cannot be resolved, preserve them as unresolved rather than guessing.

## 4. CRITICAL REVIEW — mandatory counter-test
Before closure, challenge every material claim/hypothesis including initial impressions and accepted HUMAN claims. Search for counterexamples and plausible alternatives using comparable samples; record actual actions and supporting/opposing evidence even when none is found. Distinguish VERIFIED, PARTIAL, REFUTED, UNRESOLVED. Observations and interpretations are separate. Reject unsupported factual claims, avoid causation from correlation, no hindsight, no duplicate underlying evidence, no source-count voting. Reconcile scope, provider/metric definition, date, sample size and venue/strength differences; if conflicts persist, show both. Record exactly what changed from baseline and why. No change based only on a human author's authority. Independent verification of a published tactical observation may be impossible; label uncertainty and do not turn it into a verified fact.

## 5. STOP AND REPORT
Stop when further permitted research is unlikely to materially improve understanding or a documented access/time/evidence limit prevents progress. Never claim exhaustion unless justified. A usable READY report requires actual source-backed baseline for BOTH teams, completed logged HUMAN discovery, recorded autonomous inquiry (or justified no material follow-up), counter-tests on each material claim and explicit limitations. If a required stage is incomplete, mark PARTIAL/BLOCKED with reasons; do not publish as completed. Missing advanced metrics or genuine no HUMAN coverage may be a limitation of a READY report if all research stages were actually executed and enough reliable evidence remains. Weak or absent basic data are not READY.

Report full fixture (not pick): verified facts and underlying statistics, material research findings, contradictions, opposing evidence, plausible conditional match scenarios (not forecast), uncertainties and research/evidence audit. Consider mechanisms relevant to all seven outcomes 1/X/2, O/U2.5, BTTS Yes/No without selecting, ordering, assigning scores or probabilities. Present no analyst lean, recommendations, predicted score, A/100, confidence number or market grades. No fixed length or forced charts; use tables when they clarify actual evidence. Include at least one genuine English finding (up to three) for site display, not a forecast.

## Fail-closed quality gate
Before marking READY, confirm all of the following with actual records: startup and departmental evidence separation verified; pre-match temporal integrity; baseline frozen before HUMAN; both teams covered; all applicable HUMAN sources actually attempted and logged, permitted claims and any incidental exposure recorded; autonomous research log or substantiated no-question justification; all material hypotheses and material findings linked to actual counter-tests; claim/source trace and missing/conflict disclosures; whole-match coverage; no prohibited fields/content; no fake actions. Use schemas/analyst_report.md and run tools/validate_analyst_v3.mjs on the structured audit. A failed/unavailable validator, absent critical evidence, or unverified required research action forbids READY and completed site save; retain completed research as PARTIAL and continue the batch. Execute the validator using any authorized JS runtime (Node CLI or the exact function with the actual audit in a code tool); do not require a particular machine. No tool execution is required at startup. Structural validation cannot prove semantic accuracy, truthfulness, or actual browsing: those remain required independent review. Record the validator's actual result; never self-assert that a tool ran. Site/FINAL integration must refuse non-READY or old-version new reports; live automatic gate deployment is a separate engineering task, not implied by these repository rules.

Only after the report is frozen and gate passed may the same ANALYST switch to delivery mode or use a separate routing context to attach verified existing run IDs/market/selection metadata. No revision to research based on routing. Keep all historical versions. Evaluate the new method prospectively and through time-safe backtests, comparing baseline-only vs HUMAN-enhanced findings, coverage, counter-test quality, contamination and downstream results. No performance gain is claimed before validation.
