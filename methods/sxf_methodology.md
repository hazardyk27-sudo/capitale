# SXF Methodology

Status: DRAFT
Version: 0.1

## Objective

Interpret SmartXFlow-only money and odds behavior without contamination from team analysis, current news, or Polymarket.

## Current approved scope

SXF may describe:
- when money arrived relative to kickoff;
- which market/selection received flow;
- money size and concentration;
- whether odds moved with or against the flow;
- whether movement persisted or reversed;
- whether related markets confirm or conflict.

## Not yet approved

The following must remain unset until the owner completes SmartXFlow filter research:
- minimum money/volume thresholds;
- timing buckets and precedence;
- sharp/public classification thresholds;
- fake-sharp/reversal definitions;
- label hierarchy;
- cross-market confirmation rules;
- signal-strength scoring;
- veto/override rules.

## Temporary operating rule

Until those decisions are approved, SXF should return descriptive evidence and uncertainty only. It may use candidate labels as annotations but must not present them as calibrated or canonical signals.
