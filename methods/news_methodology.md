# NEWS v2.0 — Autonomous pre-match news research
Status: CANONICAL
Version: 2.0
Supersedes v1.6 scoring/research for new runs. Historical evidence immutable.

## 0. Preflight
Verify neutral fixture, both clubs, competition, countries, kickoff and actual independent pre-kickoff AS_OF. Use only evidence demonstrably available by cutoff. For historical replays require valid historical sources/snapshots; no later information. No selection, odds, other-role evidence or statistical performance models as research input. Incidental exposure: log source/category, disregard and rework affected conclusions. No separate context or worker required. Site and validator are later-stage checks.

## 1. Broad scan and NEWS COMPLETENESS
Search both clubs' official channels, original domestic/local beat reporting in local language, organizer and relevant international corroboration under the directory. Cover eight families for each club: MANAGER (press conference, tactical intentions, pressure); FANS_STADIUM (exceptional disruption/restrictions); LOCAL_PRESS (substantiated training, dressing-room and local developments); PLAYERS (direct statements); MATCH_STAKES (concrete competition consequences and actions, not generic motivation); CLUB_OFF_FIELD (management, dispute, institutional event); SQUAD (new absences, returns, suspension, adaptation); PREPARATION (training, rest, travel, congestion, material weather/pitch). Shared stories can cover both teams but log actual team coverage. Use event-driven relevant time windows and explain scope; older continuing issues only if current relevance established. No universal freshness cutoff. Record actual searches, queries, timestamps, results, original sources and alternatives. No material story found is acceptable after real searches. NOT_SEARCHED is not NO_MATERIAL_NEWS; missing viable alternatives or critical coverage gaps forbid READY. A blocked individual site does not block research. No all-domain quota.

## 2. Material discovery
Filter routine supporter transport/buses, ordinary meetings, tickets/sellouts, commonplace choreography, generic PR, isolated sentiment, generic motivational quotes and unrelated news. A real event may be material if it concretely changes preparation, selection, match operations, conditions or organizational/psychological circumstances. Do not demand a proven outcome impact to retain a verified material event; label possible mechanism unknown if evidence is insufficient. Unsupported rumor is an inquiry lead, not a verified fact. Cluster common underlying causes as one H#; repeated reports from same origin not independent. Record rejected leads and reasons. Do not limit research to injuries.

## 3. Autonomous investigation and RESEARCH DEPTH
Generate match-specific questions from news, contradictions, uncertainty and interactions. Follow new questions as they arise, without fixed checklist/quota. Example: verify a reported absence; distinguish new from long-standing, check actual date, original statement, possible replacement, confirmed training and adaptation, and conflicting reports. For manager/player statements verify speaker, original context and whether action followed; do not confuse intention with execution. For fan action verify organizer, scope and concrete operational impact, not collective psychology based on a few posts. For club crises distinguish confirmed actions from rumors; for match significance distinguish actual stakes/actions from rhetoric. Seek original source, independent corroboration where available and corrections before cutoff. Search competing explanations, mitigation and opposing evidence. If no material leads emerge, document actual screening and why further feasible research would add little. No invented leads.

## 4. Verification and mechanisms
Record source tier N1–N4 per claim, author/issuer, URL, publication/update and retrieval times, original provenance, availability by AS_OF. N1 official statement proves what was said/done, not an inferred performance effect; N2 original credible reporting, N3 contextual sentiment with caveats, N4 rumor/lead only. VERIFIED/WELL_SUPPORTED facts need traceable N1/N2 support (or appropriate corroborated evidence; N3 alone not sufficient for material facts); DISPUTED/UNRESOLVED remain clearly marked and cannot be display facts. Separate fact from interpretation; mechanism status SUPPORTED/PLAUSIBLE/UNKNOWN, not probability or score. Explain a conditional causal chain: development → concrete change → potential effect on preparation/selection/football → limiting conditions/countereffects. Address BOTH teams, potential effects on attack, defense, tempo, ability to score/concede, match operations when relevant. No automatic translation of a player's absence into a winner or goals prediction. Do not use ANALYST statistics or attempt to measure the effect numerically; genuine factual numbers and dates are allowed. No seven-outcome scoring matrix or forced directional claims. If effect unknown, say so; evidence of absence is not absence of evidence.

## 5. RESEARCH CLOSURE
Review all team/topic coverage, access gaps and alternatives, original attribution, corrections, all material leads and counterevidence. Each material lead RESOLVED (investigated, not necessarily confirmed), EXHAUSTED (actual attempts and genuine noncritical limits), or OPEN. OPEN material or critical unresolved gap means PARTIAL; session/batch limits with important work outstanding mean PARTIAL. Do not close because a story count was reached. READY may have NO_MATERIAL_NEWS only after sufficient actual both-team coverage; distinguish NO_MATERIAL_NEWS, MATERIAL_NEWS and limited evidence. No reliable basic coverage means PARTIAL/BLOCKED. Preserve completed work and next actions. Full evidence report includes all source/search/inquiry/countertest/coverage/closure records. Structural gate must actually execute; factual and semantic review remains mandatory. No validator runtime at startup required; at closure unavailable execution means PARTIAL/validation pending. No live backend automatic gate implied.

## 6. Delivery
Freeze complete READY Markdown, then same NEWS enters delivery mode and attaches verified existing fixture/run routing. No research changes based on selected market. Save via own real admin editor, N null, actual AS_OF, full report, sources, 1–3 English factual findings or documented no-material-news coverage statement. Confirm SAVED & VERIFIED and report ID/version; download and verify complete .md. Distinguish research and delivery status. Do not save incomplete as complete. No new agents, service, score or fixed quota. Post-match review separate, never change original.

## NEWS v2.0.1 — mandatory single-fixture completion lock (ACTIVE, 2026-11-24)

These acceptance rules override any older permission to move to the next fixture after a PARTIAL first scan. The research method and evidence-only output remain NEWS v2.0; new audits require protocol_revision "2.0.1".

- **One active fixture.** Process the owner-supplied order, unless the owner requests a different order. Do not issue research queries for later fixtures or prepare a batch of shallow scans while the active fixture has viable material research remaining. Keep other fixtures QUEUED (not researched, not PARTIAL). Do not use batch size, a first search pass, number of stories, arbitrary time target, summary creation, or the desire to answer quickly as a stopping reason. Work through the active fixture's outstanding actions now.
- **Actual research receipts.** Record each performed search at retrieval, with team, family, stage, query/URL, language, time and concrete observed result. Open the actual source body for every material fact; snippets/headlines or copied links are discovery only. Record source access, a verifiable locator and the relevant passage/paraphrase; do not invent timestamp precision, access, quote, source or search. Evidence IDs must trace to actual searches; no retroactive fabricated logs. Shared queries can support several families only with separately evidenced team/family results, not cloned generic descriptions.
- **Coverage is compulsory, not a quota.** For both clubs cover all 16 team/family cells; additionally document each club's direct official route, each club's original local-language route and the relevant organizer route. Record real attempts, usable findings and alternatives. A blocked publisher or empty search never by itself proves NO_MATERIAL_NEWS. For NO_MATERIAL_NEWS document reviewed results and why no material event was found. LIMITED requires actual alternative attempt or specific defensible absence of an alternative; critical gaps cannot pass. No all-domain scan or fixed news count.
- **Close the story, not just find it.** Each material story needs linked material lead(s), actual new follow-up and a distinct opposing/mitigation search, with source-linked results. First-pass searches cannot be relabelled as follow-up/counterevidence. Test the original event, current relevance, specific mechanisms and plausible mitigation. Investigate new material questions until resolved or genuinely exhausted after real alternatives; no fake closure, no generic "unknown" in place of feasible work. Each unresolved material claim is explicitly limited, not a verified finding. No forced story/question quota; if sufficient real screening finds no stories, document why further feasible searches would add little.
- **Full report gate.** Produce the complete Turkish Markdown with all mandated sections, source/search ledger, 16 coverage rows, story/inquiry/lead records, closure and embedded final JSON audit; actually run the current validator on that Markdown, perform semantic/factual review and record the genuine result. Fix failures and rerun. A self-written PASS label, a JSON-only skeleton, a summary, a ZIP of first scans or an unfinished report is not completion. Structural PASS is necessary but not proof that searches or facts are real. READY only after all checks; do not announce "finished" or "completed" before the full artifact exists and actual validation succeeds.
- **Unavoidable blocker only.** If a genuine tool/session/source limitation prevents further important work, preserve the active fixture as PARTIAL/BLOCKED with actual attempts, exact open questions and next actions. Do not call the batch finished, fabricate completion, or move to the next fixture by default. Resume the active fixture when possible; advancing past an incomplete fixture requires explicit owner instruction. Real constraints cannot be overridden. Later fixtures remain QUEUED. Do not require site/validator availability before starting research.
- **Delivery.** Save only READY to own real browser editor; verify ID/version and download where available. If browser/export is unavailable, retain and provide the complete validated Markdown as an actual file when file creation is available; label delivery separately, not SAVED/VERIFIED. A real delivery-only blocker does not invalidate completed research or prevent the next fixture once the full validated artifact has been provided. Never claim a save/download/file that did not occur. FINAL rejects new NEWS without current revision, complete report, actual PASS and research acceptance. No scores, new agents, new services or change to other department decision methods.
